About Us | हमारे बारे में

A salon built around
quiet luxury

Tucked above Chandani Chowk on VIP Road, Jawed Habib Hair and Beauty Salon was built on a simple idea: every client should leave feeling more like themselves — only better.

चांदनी चौक, वीआईपी रोड (एचडीएफसी बैंक के सामने) पर स्थित हमारा सैलून एक ही सोच पर बना है — हर ग्राहक यहाँ से पहले से बेहतर और आत्मविश्वासी बनकर जाए।

From precision haircuts to bridal beauty, our stylists bring years of training and an eye for detail to every chair. We work with premium products and an unhurried pace, in a space designed to feel calm the moment you walk in.
