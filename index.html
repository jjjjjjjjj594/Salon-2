About Lumière

A salon built around
quiet luxury

Tucked above Chandani Chowk on VIP Road, Lumière Salon was built on a simple idea: every client should leave feeling more like themselves — only better.

From precision haircuts to bridal beauty, our stylists bring years of training and an eye for detail to every chair. We work with premium products, sanitised tools and an unhurried pace, in a space designed to feel calm the moment you walk in.
